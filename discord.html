modern pet pigeon society!

The Modern Pet Pigeon Society Discord server was created by Second Chance Pigeon Rescue's founder. MPPS is a pet pigeon server with an educational focus, encouraging new and prospective owners to provide the most accurate care possible, following scientific and consistent anecdotal evidence. Join for friendly guidance, respectful discussion, and a welcoming environment, and most of all, learning to promote the ethical treatment of pigeons. Pigeons deserve a community that cares!
