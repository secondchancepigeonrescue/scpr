every dollar goes to their care

Second Chance Pigeon Rescue runs entirely on personal funds and optional donations. Every pigeon that comes to us needs a vet exam, shelter, care, and a warm, safe place to recover, and many need much more than that. Your donation goes straight to the birds in our care.

Not sure what your gift can do? Here is what different amounts cover.

What can your donation do?

$5

A Leg Band

Pays for one custom rescue leg band, so a bird can be identified for life.

$10

Food for ~4 months

A third of the cost for a bag of seed or pellets; supplies enough food for a bird for around four months.

$25

Basic Medical Supplies

Pays for a mix of medical supplies: items for wound care, parasite treatment, and/or medications.

$50

New Cage & Supplies

One new rescue cage and two bowls for food and water for another intake.

$80

A Vet Exam

One exam with our avian veterinarian for a sick or injured bird.

$100

A New Intake

A vet exam, a leg band, and about 5 to 6 months of food for one new bird.

$200

Vet Exam & Diagnostics

Vet examination and additional diagnostics for a new intake.

$400

Incubator

Pays for a high quality incubator to use for debilitated intakes needing supplemental warmth.

*Amounts are estimates based on our current costs. Donations are used when needed.
