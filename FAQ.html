FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

common questions

What species does SCPR take in?

We ONLY take in domestic rock doves, otherwise known as pigeons, with plans to set up enclosures for dove intake. We do not provide support or rehabilitation to native species of wildlife.

If you are located in Ohio and find a native bird species in need of help, please find a rehabilitator through the Ohio Wildlife Rehabilitators Association.


Do you accept owner surrenders?

We do not accept owner surrenders except in cases of emergency. Details regarding the emergency are never shared with the public. If you require emergency surrender, please contact us as soon as possible. For quicker response time, please message us through Facebook Messenger.


I found a pigeon. What do I do?

Please read our article I Found An Injured Pigeon for more information on how to help a downed, injured, ill, or sick pigeon. We allow contact for more assistance.

If we are unable to assist you, please post the pigeon and your location to the Palomacy Help Group for Pigeon & Dove Rescue & Adoption.

If the pigeon has been attacked by a cat, please contact the closest pigeon-friendly rescue and post on Palomacy Help Group (Facebook) immediately.


adoption questions

Are pigeons good pets?
Pigeons are great pets for many individuals who prefer a more gentle and quieter pet compared to parrots. They still coo loudly, make messes, and need daily care. However, they have unique personalities, can be trained to do simple tricks, and can live just as long or longer as other pets.


I have dogs/cats. Can I still have a pet pigeon?
Yes! Many owners own multiple dogs and/or cats and still own pigeons safely. It requires proper predator-proofing of the pigeon's living space, and careful supervision when the birds are out of their enclosure. We recommend absolutely zero predator-prey interaction, and free roaming your bird(s) in areas where your other animals are not free roamed. Additionally, we do not recommend free roaming your pigeons with any other pets, including all other bird species including other dove species, parrots, rabbits, etcera.


What do you require to adopt?
At a minimum, potential adopters must be over the age of 18 or have the consent of a parent or guardian (who must fill out the application).

Can I let my pigeons fly freely outside?
No. Pigeons adopted from SCPR cannot be free flown in any capacity without a secure and approved harness and leash. Free flying is dangerous and in many cases, cruel, as many birds end up lost, injured, sick, too weak to fly, or dead.
