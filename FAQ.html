FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

common questions

What species does SCPR take in? We ONLY take in domestic rock doves, otherwise known as pigeons, with plans to set up enclosures for domestic dove intake. We do not provide support or rehabilitation to native species of wildlife. If you are located in Ohio and find a native bird species in need of help, please find a rehabilitator through the Ohio Wildlife Rehabilitators Association.

Do you accept owner surrenders? We do not accept owner surrenders except in cases of emergency. Details regarding the emergency are never shared with the public. If you require emergency surrender, please contact us as soon as possible. For quicker response time, please message us through Facebook Messenger.

I found a pigeon. What do I do? Please read our article I Found An Injured Pigeon for more information on how to help a downed, injured, ill, or sick pigeon. We allow contact for more assistance. If we are unable to assist you, please post the pigeon and your location to the Palomacy Help Group for Pigeon & Dove Rescue & Adoption. If the pigeon has been attacked by a cat, please contact the closest pigeon-friendly rescue and post on Palomacy Help Group (Facebook) immediately.

Can I come visit the rescue? No. Unfortunately, we do not have a physical public location for visitors or adopters.


adoption questions

Are pigeons good pets? Pigeons are great pets for many individuals who prefer a more gentle and quieter pet compared to parrots. They still coo loudly, make messes, and need daily care. However, they have unique personalities, can be trained to do simple tricks, and can live just as long or longer as other pets.

I have dogs/cats. Can I still have a pet pigeon? Yes! Many owners own multiple dogs and/or cats and still own pigeons safely. It requires proper predator-proofing of the pigeon's living space, and careful supervision when the birds are out of their enclosure. We recommend absolutely zero predator-prey interaction, and free roaming your bird(s) in areas where your other animals are not free roamed. Additionally, we do not recommend free roaming your pigeons with any other pets, including all other bird species including other dove species, parrots, rabbits, etc.

What do you require to adopt? Potential adopters must be 18 years old or older, or have the consent of a parent or guardian (who must fill out the application). You can apply via our . Adopter's are required to keep their birds in a secure enclosure and home, no indoor smoking, and provide adequate care. Additionally, pigeons from our rescue cannot be free flown, bred, shown in pigeon shows, and should not be abandoned or dumped.

What if I can't keep my bird? If you're unable to keep your bird any longer, regardless of the reason, SCPR will always take back a bird from any adopter. Please contact us if you cannot keep your bird so we can arrange transport.


pigeon questions

Don't pigeons carry diseases? Contrary to popular belief, pigeons are not as disease-ridden as many claim they are. Additionally, out of the diseases and parasites pigeons can carry, zoonotic diseases are low risk to transmit to humans. The highest risk comes from inhaling or ingesting dust and feces.

How long do pigeons live? Pigeons live on average 10 to 15 years, with some individual birds living over 20 years old. If you plan to adopt a pigeon, plan to accomodate for them for many years!
